# Reach Deck font bundle — license notes

The TTF/OTF inputs are committed in [`sources/`](sources/), and the WOFF2 files next to this
document are generated web artifacts from those sources. Name-table copyright and license records
are preserved. The source manifest, hashes and complete notices live in
[`sources/manifest.json`](sources/manifest.json),
[`sources/SHA256SUMS`](sources/SHA256SUMS) and [`sources/licenses/`](sources/licenses/).

The OFL permits bundling, subsetting, and web serving as long as the fonts are not sold standalone
and reserved font names are respected. The subsets keep the original family/license name records.

Alibaba PuHuiTi is not OFL: its official materials state free commercial use, while its name table
says “All rights reserved” and its redistribution/re-hosting terms are less explicit than the open
licenses. See the bundled Alibaba notice before distributing outside ReachAny-controlled products
or registries. The old Liberation binaries likewise must not be described as current Liberation
OFL releases; their historical GPLv2 exceptions and source archive are recorded with the sources.
